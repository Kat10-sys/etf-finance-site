TFSA & RRSP Contribution Room Calculator

Estimates your available room using published CRA dollar limits and the numbers you enter — not a substitute for your official CRA record.

This is an estimate, not your official contribution room. The CRA is the only authoritative source — sign in to CRA My Account or check your latest Notice of Assessment before contributing. Over-contributing to a TFSA or RRSP can trigger a 1%-per-month penalty tax on the excess amount. This tool does not know about non-residency years, forgotten contributions/withdrawals, or pension adjustments you haven't entered — it can only be as accurate as what you tell it. A withdrawal only adds back to your room starting January 1 of the following year — don't include a withdrawal you made earlier this year, since it isn't available to recontribute until next year.

RRSP Contribution Room

New room is 18% of last year's earned income, up to the year's dollar maximum, plus whatever you're carrying forward.

RRSP contributions can generally be made until December 31 of the year you turn 71. If you belong to an employer pension or deferred profit-sharing plan, your pension adjustment appears on your T4 slip — leave it at 0 if this doesn't apply to you.
